# Elaine Marrow

The traveller. She arrived in the city on a leaking ferry with the relic sewn into the lining of her cloak, carrying a sealed letter she had not yet opened and a name she had been told to find: [[Magda Vos]].

## Background

Born in the flat country to the south. Trained as a courier before she understood what it was she would be asked to carry. The order noticed her because she was quick, and quiet, and because she did not ask questions.

## Notes

She is braver than she believes, and less prepared than she hopes. The road north will test her.

## Appearance

Tall, weathered, dark-haired. Wears a grey travelling cloak that has seen better years.
