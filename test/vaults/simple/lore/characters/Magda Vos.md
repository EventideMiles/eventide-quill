# Magda Vos

[[the traveller|Elaine Marrow]] came to the city to find [[Magda Vos]]. The scholar was old beyond reckoning, keeper of the relic and watcher of the order's slow return. She had not left her tall narrow house in seven years, and the dust of the city had settled over her windowsills like a grey and patient snow.

## Background

Once a professor at the university, [[Magda Vos]] abandoned her post after the first dispersal and has lived since in the steep quarter above the harbour, studying what others feared to name. She is rumoured to have been the last person to see the relic whole.

## Notes

Her ties to [[the order]] are older than her ties to the city itself. She does not speak of them willingly.
