# Saltmere

The city. Grey rooftops, steep streets, a harbour that has not known a prosperous tide in a generation. The scholar's tall narrow house stands in the quarter above the water, pressed between two larger buildings, its door painted black.

## Notes

[[Magda Vos]] has lived here for seven years without leaving. The order has agents in every quarter. The travellers who arrive by ferry are watched from the moment their feet touch the stones.

The village to the north, where the relic must finally be carried, is not marked on any map of the city's making.
