# The Order of the Quiet Door

The order. Old, patient, and very nearly invisible until it moves. Its members have wanted the relic returned for longer than [[Magda Vos]] has been alive, and they are willing to kill to reclaim it.

## Notes

The order believes the relic is a key. The scholar believes the same. They disagree only on whether the door it opens should be opened.
