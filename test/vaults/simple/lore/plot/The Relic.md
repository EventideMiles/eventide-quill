# The Relic

A small, cool shape wrapped in older linen. Lighter than it looks. Listens, or seems to. The order calls it a key; the scholar calls it a responsibility. The traveller, in her darker moments, calls it a burden.

## Notes

It does nothing. That, in the scholar's view, is the most alarming thing about it.
